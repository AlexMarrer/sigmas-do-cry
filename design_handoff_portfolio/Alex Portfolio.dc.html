About me

Hoi, I'm Alex.

I'm a software developer from Basel, Switzerland — the kind who genuinely enjoys both designing a database schema and nudging a border-radius by two pixels.

Over the last six-plus years I've built booking platforms, dashboards, mobile apps and CMS-driven sites — mostly on .NET, Java and Angular. I like long-lived projects: software you maintain, refine, and still like two years later.

When I'm not shipping, I'm usually somewhere with a camera and a train ticket. The gallery has the evidence.
